## The Opportunity

Small business owners need fast, confident access to their cash positions and the ability to authorize urgent payroll transfers in seconds from their phone. The existing app required navigating four layers of nested menus just to review pending approvals.

<Callout type="warning" title="User Friction">
Over 60% of business users surveyed reported abandoning payments on mobile and waiting until they were back in front of a desktop computer due to poor visual confirmation of high-value transfers.
</Callout>

## Design Process

1. **Contextual Inquiries**: Observed 12 small business operators handling day-to-day operations between customer inquiries.
2. **Rapid Prototyping**: Iterated on biometric confirmation flows and batch-action approval trays.
3. **Usability Testing**: Tested interactive Figma prototypes with realistic mock accounting transactions.

## Results

- **3.5x faster** completion time for multi-account payment authorization.
- App store rating climbed from 3.2 to 4.7 stars within three months of release.
